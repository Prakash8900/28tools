Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between GST inclusive and exclusive?
GST Inclusive means the tax is already added to the total amount. GST Exclusive means tax needs to be added onto the base price.
How are CGST and SGST calculated?
For intra-state transactions in India, the total GST is split equally: 50% goes to Central GST (CGST) and 50% to State GST (SGST).
Can I use custom GST percentage rates?
Yes, select 'Custom...' in the rate dropdown to input any custom tax percentage like 14% or 0.25%.
