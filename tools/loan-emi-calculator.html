Frequently Asked Questions

What is the EMI calculation formula?
EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P is Principal, R is monthly interest rate, and N is the number of monthly installments.
Does increasing loan tenure reduce monthly EMI?
Yes, a longer tenure reduces the monthly EMI amount, but it increases the total interest you will pay over the life of the loan.
