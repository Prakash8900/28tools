Frequently Asked Questions

What is a SIP?
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly in mutual fund schemes to benefit from rupee cost averaging and compounding.
Are SIP returns guaranteed?
Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. The calculator provides mathematical estimates based on your chosen rate.
