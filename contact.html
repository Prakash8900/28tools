Frequently Asked Questions

🐛 How do I report a bug?

Select "Bug Report" in the subject and describe what tool you were using, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened. Screenshots are helpful!

💡 Can I request a new tool?

Absolutely! We love hearing what tools our users need. Select "Feature Request" and describe what you'd like to see built.

🔒 Is my message private?

Yes. We will only use your email address to reply to your message. We never sell or share contact information with third parties.

⏱️ How long until a reply?

We typically respond within 1–3 business days. For urgent issues, please include "URGENT" in your subject line.
