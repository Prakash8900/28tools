About 28Tools

Engineered for speed, privacy, and simplicity — free forever.

28Tools is an all-in-one suite of 32 free, high-performance online utility tools designed to make everyday computing tasks effortless. Whether you need to calculate loan EMIs, convert currencies, resize photos, merge PDF documents, generate secure passwords, or format JSON data, 28Tools delivers instant results directly within your web browser.

Our Core Mission

Most online conversion and calculation websites are cluttered with intrusive popups, mandatory registration forms, and slow server queues that upload your private documents to unknown external servers.

At 28Tools, our mission is simple: privacy-first, browser-local computing. We leverage cutting-edge WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and modern JavaScript APIs so your files and sensitive numerical data never leave your computer or smartphone.

🔒
100% Client-Side Privacy
Your files and financial data are computed in your browser memory. Nothing is uploaded to remote servers.
⚡
Zero Lag & Instant Output
No server queue delays. Operations like image compression and tax calculation happen at the speed of your processor.
💎
Always Free & No Signup
Every tool is completely free with no usage limits, credit cards, or account creation required.
📱
Mobile & Touch Friendly
Responsive layouts carefully optimized for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide desktop monitors.
